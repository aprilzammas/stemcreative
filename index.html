My name is April Zammas and I’m a graphic & production designer with 20+ years across brand, marketing, web, and email, plus a slightly unfair advantage: four years as a Senior Software Engineer at Capital One. I bridge creative execution and technical systems, and I’m a detail-oriented, fast yet efficient team player with a long history of thriving under pressure and easing that pressure with a good joke or two.

Ask anyone what I do and they’ll often say I “make things pretty” or “make things work correctly.” That’s me in a nutshell. Branding and content management are my passion. Print is where my career began, but I’m equally invested in digital: web interface, email at scale, social, content management systems, and e-commerce product setup. Most recently I’ve been designing for big-box home improvement retailers.

I teach myself complex tools, build repeatable systems, and train others to use them. I also work with AI daily (Figma, Lovable, ChatGPT, Claude, Adobe Firefly) to design, prototype, and ship faster without sacrificing craft.
